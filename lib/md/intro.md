## Personal summary

Hello! :) I am Dávid Kepič, also known as dejvokep, a junior software engineer with a background in math, physics, and
IT; passionate about thinking-intensive tasks and teamwork. I am currently studying Computer Engineering at the Faculty
of Information Technology of CTU in Prague.

## How did I get here

My software engineering journey started early on playing with the intelligent LEGO EV3 set and while dreaming of
becoming a Minecraft server owner. To be honest, I did not even own my own license, until I was gifted one for my help
on a server of my friend. Funnily enough, my projects of this era are still available on marketplaces and open-sourced
on GitHub. And yet, I am now proudly continuing as a productive individual helping to support systems
serving thousands of users daily, from SSL/TLS certificates and load balancers, all the way to raw HTML, CSS and server
code.

Having designed and produced my first PCB at the age of 14, I have always shown hands-on interest in hardware and system
design alongside software. I remember building my own computer when the pandemic hit and watching the release of
the RTX 3000 series, doing research and watching countless videos and leaks. Since then, my academic studies have
broadened my knowledge not only in concepts of linear algebra and algorithms, but also in the HDL domains and CPU
architectures.

## In my free time

Outside of work and school I contribute to open-source projects, travel and enjoy spending time with people - habits and
activities that keep me sharp and curious. Volunteering has a special place in my heart and sometimes I and my team
attend hackathons, the submissions for many of which I am very proud of.
